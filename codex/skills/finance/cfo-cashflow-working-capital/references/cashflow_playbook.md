# Cash-flow Playbook

## 13-week forecast structure

A 13-week forecast should be direct, not only accrual-based. It should show expected cash receipts and cash payments by week.

Recommended categories:

- Opening cash.
- Customer receipts by source and certainty.
- Tax refunds / grants / financing receipts.
- Payroll.
- Rent / facilities.
- Critical suppliers.
- Non-critical suppliers.
- Inventory purchases.
- Debt service.
- Tax payments.
- CapEx.
- One-off items.
- Closing cash.
- Minimum cash threshold.
- Breach date.

## Cash certainty ladder

Classify inflows as:

1. Cash already received.
2. Invoice due and confirmed by customer.
3. Invoice issued but not confirmed.
4. Contracted but not invoiced.
5. Pipeline / uncertain.

Do not treat uncertain inflows as reliable survival cash.

## Cash levers by time horizon

### 0-2 weeks

- Freeze discretionary payments.
- Daily bank reconciliation.
- Top 20 AR calls.
- Approve only critical AP.
- Negotiate supplier holds.
- Confirm payroll and tax dates.

### 2-13 weeks

- Accelerate billing.
- Change payment terms.
- Inventory liquidation plan.
- Receivables financing evaluation.
- Customer deposit policy.
- Short-term shareholder/bank facility.

### 3-12 months

- Pricing and margin correction.
- Procurement renegotiation.
- Production planning changes.
- Contract structure changes.
- Permanent working capital facility.
