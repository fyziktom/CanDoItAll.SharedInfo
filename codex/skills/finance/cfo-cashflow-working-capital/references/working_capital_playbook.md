# Working Capital Playbook

## Core formulas

- DSO = Accounts receivable / Revenue × Period days.
- DIO = Inventory / Cost of goods sold × Period days.
- DPO = Accounts payable / Cost of goods sold × Period days.
- Cash conversion cycle = DSO + DIO - DPO.
- Net working capital = AR + Inventory - AP.

## Diagnosis patterns

- DSO up + revenue up: billing/collections cannot keep pace with growth.
- DSO up + revenue flat: customer quality, disputes, or collection discipline problem.
- DIO up + gross margin down: inventory obsolescence or pricing pressure risk.
- DPO up + supplier complaints: company may be borrowing from suppliers involuntarily.
- CCC up while EBITDA positive: accounting profit is not converting to cash.

## CFO actions

- Segment AR by customer, age, dispute reason, and collection owner.
- Put the largest overdue accounts into weekly executive review.
- Separate critical suppliers from deferrable suppliers.
- Review reorder points, MOQ commitments, and slow-moving stock.
- Tie sales incentives partly to collected cash or clean order quality where appropriate.
- Align purchase commitments with realistic demand and cash capacity.
