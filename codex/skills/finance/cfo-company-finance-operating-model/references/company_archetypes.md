# Company Archetypes and Finance Priorities

## Product company

Needs SKU profitability, gross margin bridge, inventory control, warranty accruals, supply-chain risk monitoring, and purchase commitment visibility.

## Manufacturing company

Needs standard costing, variance analysis, capacity utilization, yield, scrap, maintenance capex, production planning, inventory aging, and supplier concentration review.

## SaaS company

Needs ARR/MRR, retention, churn, CAC, LTV, gross margin by product, cloud cost allocation, deferred revenue, billing reliability, and customer cohort economics.

## Professional services company

Needs utilization, realization, project margin, WIP, timesheet discipline, contract scope control, subcontractor margin, and milestone billing.

## Project business

Needs contract-level P&L, WIP, claims/change orders, milestone cash collection, retention, project risk reserves, and project manager accountability.

## Holding / group company

Needs legal entity reporting, consolidated view, intercompany balances, treasury, cash pooling, transfer pricing review, shared-service allocation, and governance by entity.

## Non-profit / grant-funded entity

Needs restricted fund accounting, donor reporting, program cost allocation, grant compliance, cash restrictions, and reputational risk controls.

## Regulated financial entity

Needs capital adequacy, liquidity risk, compliance, model risk, risk committees, segregation of duties, customer money protection, and robust governance.
