# Finance Operating Model Checklist

## Minimum viable finance model

- Bank reconciliation process.
- Monthly close owner and calendar.
- Cash forecast owner and cadence.
- Basic chart of accounts.
- Cost centers for departments.
- Approval matrix for spending.
- Billing process and AR aging review.
- AP payment run process.
- Payroll control.
- Basic management report.

## Scaleup model

- Budget owners and monthly variance reviews.
- Product/project/customer profitability.
- Working capital KPIs.
- Investment approval process.
- Contract margin review.
- Procurement policy.
- Revenue recognition review.
- Data quality controls.
- Board reporting pack.

## Enterprise / group model

- Consolidation process.
- Treasury policy.
- Intercompany agreements.
- Transfer pricing support.
- Group delegation of authority.
- Internal audit or control testing.
- Risk committee.
- Scenario planning.
- Formal capital allocation committee.
