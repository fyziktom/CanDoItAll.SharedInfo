# Reporting Pack Playbook

## Monthly pack sections

1. Executive summary.
2. P&L actual vs budget vs prior period.
3. Revenue bridge and margin bridge.
4. Cash-flow and 13-week outlook.
5. Working capital and balance sheet movements.
6. Debt and covenant status.
7. KPI dashboard.
8. Department/project/product profitability.
9. Risks and externalities.
10. Decisions requested and action log.

## Good CFO narrative

A good CFO narrative answers:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- Is it temporary or structural?
- What is the cash impact?
- What is management doing?
- What decision is needed?

## KPI quality test

A KPI is weak if no one owns it, no one can reproduce it, it cannot be reconciled, it is easy to game, or it does not influence a decision.
