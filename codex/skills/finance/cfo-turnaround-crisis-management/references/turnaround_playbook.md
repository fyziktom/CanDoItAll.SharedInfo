# Turnaround Playbook

## First 72 hours

- Confirm bank cash and restricted cash.
- Create daily cash control.
- Build first 13-week forecast.
- Identify payroll, tax, debt, and critical supplier dates.
- Freeze discretionary spend.
- Stop unapproved hiring and capex.
- Create payment approval list.
- Assign AR collection owners.
- Inform board with facts and options.
- Identify legal/restructuring review need.

## First 2 weeks

- Run supplier negotiations.
- Review customer receipts and deposits.
- Confirm covenant status.
- Prepare lender/investor communication if needed.
- Identify asset sales or bridge financing.
- Review loss-making products/customers/projects.
- Build downside cash scenario.
- Create weekly management dashboard.

## First 13 weeks

- Execute cost reset or margin correction.
- Stabilize supply chain.
- Repair billing and collection process.
- Restructure debt or raise financing.
- Redesign operating model if structural burn remains.
- Implement permanent cash controls.

## Distress warning indicators

- Payroll uncertainty.
- Tax arrears.
- Critical supplier credit holds.
- Covenant breach or waiver need.
- Deferred AP growing faster than revenue.
- AR aging deteriorating.
- Inventory cannot be converted to cash.
- Investors/lenders losing confidence.
