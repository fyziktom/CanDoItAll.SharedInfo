# Group Finance Playbook

## Management view vs legal view

The management view answers: who is responsible for performance? The legal view answers: which entity owns the obligation, asset, cash, tax exposure, and legal risk? Both views are necessary.

## Responsibility center types

- Cost center: accountable for cost control, not revenue.
- Revenue center: accountable for revenue, not full margin.
- Profit center: accountable for revenue and controllable costs.
- Investment center: accountable for returns on capital employed.
- Shared service center: provides internal services and needs service-level metrics.

## Intercompany controls

- Maintain intercompany agreement register.
- Reconcile intercompany balances monthly.
- Define settlement cadence.
- Separate trading balances from loans.
- Document management fees and cost recharges.
- Flag tax/transfer pricing review needs.
- Avoid using intercompany balances to hide poor cash collection.
