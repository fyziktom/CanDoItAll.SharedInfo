# Capital Allocation Playbook

## Investment categories

- Survival / compliance: must be done to operate legally or safely.
- Maintenance: protects existing capacity or revenue.
- Efficiency: reduces cost, working capital, or risk.
- Growth: expands capacity, customers, geography, or product scope.
- Strategic option: creates future optionality under uncertainty.
- Acquisition: buys capability, market access, cash-flow, or assets.

## Decision rules

- Reject or redesign projects that cannot explain cash impact.
- Require stage gates for uncertain growth bets.
- Separate strategic value from measurable cash-flow value.
- Include implementation capacity as a scarce resource.
- Include working capital, not only P&L impact.
- Compare against alternative uses of cash: debt reduction, runway extension, hiring, marketing, acquisitions, dividends, or reserves.
- Review actual results after implementation.

## Common CFO traps

- Ignoring installation, training, maintenance, and downtime.
- Ignoring working capital and inventory needed for growth.
- Treating projected revenue as certain.
- Excluding management attention cost.
- Accepting optimistic terminal values.
- Not modeling covenant or liquidity impact.
- Letting sunk cost drive continuation decisions.
