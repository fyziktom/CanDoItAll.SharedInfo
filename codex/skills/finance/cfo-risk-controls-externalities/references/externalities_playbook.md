# Externalities Playbook

## Externalities as CFO issues

An externality matters financially when it can affect:

- price;
- volume;
- cost;
- asset value;
- working capital;
- financing access;
- insurance;
- licenses;
- reputation;
- legal exposure;
- continuity of supply or operations.

## Materiality lenses

- Financial materiality: effects on enterprise value and cash-flow.
- Impact materiality: effects of the company on people, society, and environment.
- Dynamic materiality: today's external impact can become tomorrow's financial cost.

## CFO actions

- Identify the financial channel.
- Estimate magnitude and time horizon.
- Assign owner.
- Add early warning indicator.
- Integrate into budget, capex, insurance, supplier, and reporting processes.
