# Risk and Controls Playbook

## Control types

- Preventive: approval limits, purchase orders, access restrictions, segregation of duties.
- Detective: reconciliations, variance reviews, exception reports, inventory counts.
- Corrective: incident response, credit holds, remediation plans, insurance claims.
- Automated: workflow approvals, system validations, matching rules.
- Manual: management review, checklist, sign-off.

## Fraud and leakage examples

- Fake supplier or changed bank details.
- Duplicate invoices.
- Unapproved discounts.
- Payroll ghost employee.
- Inventory shrinkage.
- Uncontrolled software subscriptions.
- Expense abuse.
- Intercompany manipulation.

## Risk governance

- Each material risk needs an owner.
- Each owner needs an action plan.
- Each action plan needs a due date.
- High risks need board/management visibility.
- Residual risk should be explicit, not hidden.
