# Risk, Controls, and Externalities Skill

## When to use

Use this skill for enterprise risk, financial controls, fraud prevention, governance, risk register scoring, externalities, sustainability-related financial risks, climate transition/physical risks, supply-chain risk, regulatory risk, cyber/data risk, reputational risk, and resilience planning.

## CFO principle

Externalities become financial when they affect cash, margin, access to capital, insurance, licenses, customer demand, supply continuity, regulation, litigation, or reputation. Internal controls protect the company from avoidable losses, unreliable reporting, fraud, and uncontrolled commitments.

## Risk and externality workflow

1. Identify risk category:
   - strategic;
   - financial;
   - liquidity;
   - operational;
   - compliance;
   - technology / cyber;
   - supply chain;
   - people;
   - market;
   - environmental / climate;
   - social / community;
   - governance;
   - reputational.
2. Score each risk:
   - likelihood;
   - impact;
   - velocity;
   - control effectiveness;
   - time horizon;
   - owner;
   - early warning indicator.
3. Translate externalities to financial channels:
   - revenue loss;
   - cost increase;
   - capex requirement;
   - asset impairment;
   - working capital impact;
   - financing cost;
   - insurance availability/cost;
   - legal/regulatory penalty;
   - supply disruption;
   - reputational/customer churn.
4. Define controls:
   - preventive;
   - detective;
   - corrective;
   - automated;
   - manual;
   - board-level oversight.
5. Balance controls with operational friction.

## Internal control baseline

For CFO purposes, think in five layers:

1. Control environment: tone, ethics, authority, competence.
2. Risk assessment: known risks, change risks, fraud risks.
3. Control activities: approvals, reconciliations, segregation of duties, limits, evidence.
4. Information and communication: reliable data and escalation.
5. Monitoring: review, testing, remediation, audit trail.

## Externality examples

- Climate physical risk: flood, heat, storms, supply disruption, insurance cost.
- Climate transition risk: carbon pricing, energy cost, customer requirements, stranded assets.
- Social externality: labor practices, safety, community conflict, customer trust.
- Technology externality: dependency on cloud, AI provider, cybersecurity, data privacy.
- Regulatory externality: new compliance cost, licensing change, reporting requirement.
- Supply-chain externality: supplier concentration, geopolitical risk, logistics disruption.
