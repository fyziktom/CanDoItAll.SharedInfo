# Treasury Policy Playbook

## Minimum treasury policy contents

- Bank account ownership and signatory rules.
- Payment approval authority.
- Cash forecast cadence.
- Minimum liquidity threshold.
- Debt register owner.
- Covenant reporting calendar.
- FX exposure identification and hedge approval.
- Intercompany loan approval process.
- Cash pooling rules.
- Emergency funding protocol.

## Debt register fields

- Borrower legal entity.
- Lender.
- Facility type.
- Principal outstanding.
- Currency.
- Interest rate and benchmark.
- Maturity.
- Amortization schedule.
- Security/collateral.
- Guarantees.
- Covenants.
- Reporting deadlines.
- Events of default.

## Lender communication principles

- Do not surprise lenders.
- Forecast covenant issues early.
- Separate factual reporting from optimistic plans.
- Prepare credible action plan before waiver requests.
- Keep board and legal counsel aligned in distress.
